## Categorical Imperative

All actions, rules, and tools must be justifiable as the application of a general rule covering all similar cases.

- Prohibit one-off exceptions, ad-hoc workarounds, and prohibitive rules over-fitted to a single incident; escalate for general rulemaking when novel classes arise.
- Design tools and instructions to cover the broadest category admitted; never build single-use structural or migration scripts where general instructions or reusable capabilities cover the class.
