# Review Guidance

Scheme-agnostic guidance. Retrieve scheme-specific criteria, band definitions, and weights from the round's handbook.

## Feedback Taxonomy

Categorize feedback elements to ensure balanced coverage:

- **Scope**: Specific (sentence, page, quote), Section, or Document-level.
- **Type**: Structural, Methodological, Substantive, Clarity, Citation, Strategic-fit.
- **Direction**: Strength, Weakness, Gap, Concern.

## Scoring Bands & Alignment

- **Anti-drift**: Top bands are reserved for exceptional proposals (~10%); the majority fall into competent middle tiers. Distinguish competitive proposals from those lacking compelling theoretical or methodological foundations.
- **Score-comment alignment**: Align qualitative feedback with assigned score bands. Inconsistent praise in low-scoring reviews impairs applicant rejoinders and panel review.

## Tone Calibration

- **Grants**: Constructive and supportive. Focus on proposal significance, feasibility, and investigator capacity.
- **Journals**: Rigorous and critical. Focus on methodological validity and whether findings support claims.
- **Collegial reviews**: Actionable and direct; emphasize high-leverage structural improvements.

## Anti-Bias Drafting Discipline

Integrate bias mitigation directly into draft construction:

- **Focus on text**: Evaluate proposals, not individual character or status.
- **Career stage**: Frame early-career records in context (ROPE); assess opportunity-relative achievement without deficit framing.
- **Institutional critique**: Attribute resourcing constraints (e.g. teaching loads, facilities) to institutional arrangements rather than researcher performance.
- **Evidence over prestige**: Base scores strictly on proposal evidence rather than author affiliation, prestige indicators, or minor presentation flaws.

## Prohibited Content

Exclude the following from submitted assessment text:

- Explicit numerical or letter scores within prose comments.
- Generic boilerplate phrases repeated across multiple reviews.
- Narrative summaries simply restating the application without evaluation.
- Comparisons to other applications or naming third-party applicants.
- External unverified research (no searching outside provided materials).
- Eligibility decisions or research integrity allegations (route to scheme offices).
- AI-generated text violating funder GenAI policies.
