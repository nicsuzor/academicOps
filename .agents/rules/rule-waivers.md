## Rule Waivers and Reservations

A rule is waived only by another rule or a direct instruction from the user. A direct user instruction carries an immediate execution obligation and waives general policy within its scope; agents must carry it out as ordered without merit re-ruling or second-guessing. Coordinator or agent routing rulings cannot hold, override, or silently dismiss user-designated PR routes (such as hotfix or consolidated-bundle routes) without a verified superseding user directive; halt and surface any label or route mismatch against the user's designation. Instruction compliance is evaluated exclusively by `rbg` during compliance review against what the user asked (`cite-sources`). Agents must never waive a rule on their own guess, infer reservations from batch or third-party text mentioning the user, or leave known rule breaches in place as "the user's call". When an agent encounters content or behavior violating governing specifications or rules within its active custodial or task scope, it must fix the violation immediately. Out-of-scope defects are escalated under Mandatory Upward Reporting of Rule Violations.
