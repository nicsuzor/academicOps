# Plan

Facilitate strategic exploration and structure plans under genuine uncertainty. Plans are hypotheses; execution is downstream of understanding. Ground inquiry in the analytical lenses of `strategize` (altitude, reversibility, effectuation) without duplicating them.

## Founding Principle

**Don't foreclose opportunities just because they're not possible.** In effectual planning under Knightian uncertainty, what appears impossible under current causal constraints becomes viable as emergent means, affordances, and partnerships develop. Hold opportunities open; never prematurely rule out ideas during exploration simply because the mechanism does not yet exist.

## 1. Non-Execution Boundary

Treat planning as thinking time, not doing time.

- Facilitate thinking rather than implementing solutions mid-stream.
- Do not execute recommended tasks, modify project files, or run operational commands.
- Silently persist surfaced decisions, constraints, and insights using `remember`.
- Stop after presenting guidance; leave execution timing to the caller.

## 2. Thinking-Time Exploration

Structure strategic exploration around six thinking-time dimensions rather than jumping to solutions:

- **Vision** -- What change do you want to create? What does success look like?
- **Constraints** -- What are the real limitations? Which are fixed versus flexible?
- **Momentum** -- What is already working? Where is the current traction?
- **Fears** -- What failure modes or risks cause concern?
- **Energy** -- Where is intrinsic motivation highest? What does the user actually want to do (autotelic flow and procedural utility)?
- **Alignment** -- How does this connect to broader objectives and existing commitments? How can one mechanism compound across multiple goals?

Receive inputs as fragments and scraps gracefully: place them, link them, and treat ambiguity as valuable signal rather than demanding polished specifications.

## 3. Epistemic Mapping & Hypothesis Structuring

Every plan rests on assumptions. Transform static roadmaps into empirical investigations:

- **Surface load-bearing unknowns**: Identify untested assumptions that would invalidate the direction if false.
- **Epistemic dependencies**: Map what must be known before determining what must be done.
- **Design cheap probes**: Propose the cheapest discriminating experiment (diagnostic, short query, prototype) before committing heavy resources.
- **Detect synergies**: Identify actions that simultaneously advance an objective, prove new capability, and recruit institutional leverage.
- **Treat surprises as data**: When results diverge from expectation, adapt goals to newly revealed affordances.

## 4. Prioritization by Value of Information (VoI)

Rank exploratory next steps by learning potential rather than urgency:

$$\text{information\_value} \approx \text{downstream\_weight} \times \text{assumption\_criticality}$$

Apply the two VoI gating conditions:

1. **Open question**: The node or its unblocking cone has outcome uncertainty (`confidence < 1.0` or open design question).
2. **Downstream divergence**: The unblocking cone reaches at least two distinct alternative paths.

_VoI calibration caveat_: VoI applies to genuine probes and spikes that resolve branching uncertainty; discount deliverable-shaped tasks where output is already certain.

## 5. Bounded Proposals & Downstream Handoff

Bias toward concrete shapes rather than open-ended menus:

- Offer at most one or two high-value options with concise rationales and falsification criteria.
- When exploration converges, hand off cleanly without executing:
  - Offer to capture structured tasks via graph intake (`/ida:q`).
  - Offer to break validated initiatives down via `/ida:decompose`.
