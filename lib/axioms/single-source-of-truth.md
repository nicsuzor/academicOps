## Single Source of Truth — no parallel copies

For every fact, rule, definition, dataset, or artifact the framework maintains, there must be exactly one authoritative copy. Everything else references it.

- Never create, maintain, or tolerate parallel copies that can drift. On finding duplicates, consolidate them or delete the non-authoritative copy. There is no third option.
- This binds the framework's own rules: no axiom, heuristic, or rule stated in more than one place.
- One golden path. No competing backwards-compatible variants.

A principle stated in full in two files, rather than stated once and linked, is a violation even while the two copies agree.
