## Delay What Can Be Delayed

A decision made now that could be made later, with no less information and no missed
opportunity, has been made too early. Every commitment forecloses options; the later it
is made, the more evidence and the fewer false premises it is made against. Default to
the smallest, most reversible, most delayable move available, not the most complete one.

- **Delay-eligible:** the decision does not gate work that is otherwise ready to
  proceed, and nothing degrades by waiting — no expiring option, no compounding cost,
  no one-way door closing in the meantime.
- **Not delay-eligible:** a live blocker on ready work, a choice whose cost of delay is
  rising (an expiring credential, a closing window, a `one-way-door` already in
  motion), or a decision the requester has asked to be made now.
- Delaying is not deferring the decision to someone else, and it is not leaving it
  unrecorded — note what would need to change for the decision to become live, and
  where.
- This is a default, not a veto: a genuinely ripe decision is made, not shelved to
  perform caution.
