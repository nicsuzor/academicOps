# Platform-Specific Submission Instructions

## ARC Research Management System (RMS)

- URL: https://rms.arc.gov.au/RMS/Assignment/Manage
- **MFA required** — set up before deadline week.
- Accept/reject assignments first (CoI check) — do this immediately on receiving the announce email; the deadline for accept/reject is typically tighter than the assessment deadline.
- Assessor handbook: https://www.arc.gov.au/funding-research/peer-review/assessor-resources
- Quality review guide: https://www.arc.gov.au/funding-research/peer-review/how-write-quality-peer-review
- **Save frequently** — auto-save is unreliable.
- Verify PDF rendering on the proposal preview before scoring.
- Browser: Chrome/Edge most reliable. Firefox occasionally has form-field issues.
- Char counts are enforced server-side at submit. Pre-flight: paste each criterion into a counter.
- Confirmation lands by email after submission — keep that for the task file.

## ERC (EvalAccess)

- Step 2 remote referee role is the most common assignment.
- Section weights vary by call — read the specific call's evaluation guide.
- "Scientific relevance" and "ground-breaking nature" are the dominant lenses.
- Risk/feasibility balance: ERC explicitly rewards ambition; do not penalise high-risk projects on feasibility unless the team has not addressed the risk.

## SNSF mySNF

- Swiss-specific: scientific relevance section is heavily weighted.
- Track record is opportunity-adjusted — note career interruptions where flagged.

## NHMRC Sapphire (Australia)

- For grants with a methodology focus, the Approach criterion typically dominates.
- Different scoring scale than ARC — check the call's guide.

## General

- Always download a copy of the submitted assessment for the task file.
- If a platform throws an error mid-submission, screenshot before retrying — it helps support and helps you avoid duplicate submissions.

## Research Integrity

If you suspect a research integrity breach during assessment:

- **Do NOT** mention concerns in the assessment text.
- For ARC: notify researchintegrity@arc.gov.au per ARC Research Integrity Policy §5.
- Provide enough information for the integrity office to assess referral to the institution.
- Continue with the rest of the assessment as written.

## Foreign Affiliations / Financial Support

ARC applicants must declare foreign financial support and affiliations. As assessor, you do not police these — but if a clear undisclosed affiliation is evident from the application itself, that's a research integrity matter (route as above), not assessment feedback.
