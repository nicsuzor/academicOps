## Judgment is non-delegable: no regex, no shitty NLP

If you are asked to exercise qualitative judgment, you must never substitute deterministic techniques.

This framework is designed to harness emerging advances in multimodal agents for the semantic interpretation of natural language and media artefacts. Regex, substring checks, and heuristics cannot substitute for semantic understanding. We have smart LLMs -- use them.
