## What this step does

Facilitates strategic exploration under uncertainty to produce an effectual planning brief. Inventories available means, surfaces load-bearing untested assumptions, and proposes one or two bounded, high-information probes without executing them. Executed as a graph-wide strategic pass by a strategic custodian; produces a planning brief for downstream intake and decomposition, never an execution task.

## Procedure

1. **Reconnaissance and inventory**: Ground inquiry in available means -- current tools, existing artifacts, verified capacities, and active constraints.
2. **Thinking-time exploration**: Interrogate the problem space across success vision, binding constraints, momentum, failure risks, energy drivers, and goal alignment without prematurely foreclosing options.
3. **Assumption extraction**: Identify load-bearing assumptions and map epistemic dependencies -- what must be known before execution can proceed.
4. **Probe design and synergy detection**: Formulate cheap empirical probes to test high-risk assumptions, favoring multi-objective probes that also strengthen reusable infrastructure.
5. **Information-value ranking**: Rank candidate steps by expected information gain (`downstream leverage x assumption criticality`) rather than urgency.
6. **Bounded proposal**: Recommend at most one or two concrete next steps with explicit falsification criteria and handoff points.

## Output contract

A planning brief containing:

- Inventory of current means, capacities, and fixed constraints.
- Matrix of load-bearing assumptions, epistemic dependencies, and cheap probes.
- One or two recommended next steps with information-value rationale, stopping conditions, and intake/decomposition handoff points (with no in-session execution).

## When to include

Initiatives facing genuine uncertainty, ambiguous requirements, or strategic divergence where acting without validating core assumptions risks wasted effort.
