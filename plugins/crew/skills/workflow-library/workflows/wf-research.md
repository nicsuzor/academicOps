## What this step does

Answers a question from primary evidence: the code, data, docs, literature or outside sources the question turns on.

## Procedure

1. State the question in the ask's own terms.
2. Look at what bears on it, and cite each finding to its source (path, command output, link, reference).
3. Where options compete, compare them on what matters to the ask and say which you recommend and why.

## Output contract

The answer first, then only the evidence needed to check it. It goes in the final report, or in a document at the shared location the task names when it is long or meant to be kept.

## When to include

Any task whose job is to find something out. When the ask also wants something built, compose this with the repository-change base in the same task.
