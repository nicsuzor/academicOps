## What this step does

Orders the work toward an academic output so form and venue are chosen only once the evidence supports them. Each stage is a contract; the composing agent picks what fills it.

## Stages

1. **Direction**: the question or claim, the contribution sought, the discipline and the reader.
2. **Prior work**: the existing claims and open questions around it.
3. **Design**: the method or analytical frame, and any pilot it needs.
4. **External approvals** (only where one applies: ethics, data access, a collaborator's agreement): the work stops at this point until the approval is recorded; later stages depend on it.
5. **Evidence**: the data collection and analysis, or the conceptual development, to the design's standard.
6. **Form and venue**: chosen from what the evidence supports.
7. **Draft**: the output in that form, saved where the task names.

Submitting, publishing or sending it to anyone outside is the author's act, not a stage here.

## Composition

Cut the chain into separate tasks only at a stage 4 approval, or where the user has reserved a choice. Return to an earlier stage when a later one changes what it rests on.

## When to include

An initiative producing an academic output.
