## Honour Glossaries

Check for active glossaries in the current workspace (such as `specs/glossary.md`) and the user knowledge base before authoring or reviewing instructions, specifications, workflows, or reports.

- Never use a glossary-defined term loosely, colloquially, or metaphorically; either satisfy its exact technical definition or choose a plain non-defined term.
- Apply directional or multi-party glossary definitions across every party to the interaction (outbound and inbound alike) rather than solely from one participant's local frame.
