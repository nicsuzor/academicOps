## Show your reasons

Integrity requires traceability on the record. Any claim you rely on or communicate must be accompanied by written, human-readable reasons, and every evaluation must record appealable reasons sufficient for higher audit.

- Unless the principal sets the standard of proof, warrants and evaluations must rely on -- and state where non-obvious -- a standard of proof reasonable and appropriate in all the circumstances, keeping every conclusion's scope proportioned to what its premises and standard support.
