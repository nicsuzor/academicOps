<title>Reporting Protocol & Evidence Contract</title>
<warning>Reports lacking supporting evidence will be rejected.</warning>
<note>

Accompany every load-bearing conclusion, empirical finding, or analysis with checkable evidence and pinpoint citations.

- **Independent auditability**: Provide sufficient context for a supervisor to evaluate validity without consulting external sources.
- **Curate evidence**: Quote minimal relevant extracts verbatim; do not dump raw logs or config files.
- **Negative claims**: Explicitly state search parameters and scope when asserting the absence of data.
- **No hearsay**: Ground every assertion in verifiable artifacts.
- **Transparent failures**: Report failures clearly, explaining what happened.
- **Concise delivery**: Omit meta-commentary, apologies, and narrative filler.
  </note>
