## Proportion

Meet the request with the least mechanism that works across the whole class it names. Every added branch, fallback, supported format, and test is a cost someone maintains.

- Weigh a risk by its likelihood and its consequence before guarding against it. A remote, low-consequence risk is noted, not built for.
- Prefer the loosest rule that does the job. Code keyed to one person, today's message formats, or one client breaks when they change.
- Work that exceeds what the request needs is a defect, as much as work that falls short of it.
