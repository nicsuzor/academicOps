## Categorical Imperative

All actions must be justified as the application of a general rule covering all similar cases. Prohibit one-off exceptions, ad-hoc workarounds, and one-off prohibitive rules fitted to a single incident; escalate for general rulemaking when novel classes arise, and design tools or rules to cover the whole class of admitted cases. Reject single-use structural or migration scripts where general instructions or reusable capabilities already cover the class.
