## What this step does

Facilitates strategic exploration under uncertainty to produce an effectual planning brief. Maps available means, explores core tensions across thinking-time dimensions, surfaces load-bearing untested assumptions, and proposes one or two bounded, high-information next steps without executing them.

## Procedure

1. **Reconnaissance and inventory** -- Ground inquiry in available means: current tools, existing artifacts, verified capacities, and active constraints.
2. **Thinking-time exploration** -- Interrogate the strategic problem space across key dimensions: vision of success, binding constraints, traction momentum, failure fears, energy drivers, and goal alignment.
3. **Assumption extraction** -- Identify load-bearing assumptions required for the strategy to succeed. Map epistemic dependencies: determine what must be known before execution can proceed.
4. **Probe design and synergy detection** -- Formulate cheap, empirical probes to test high-risk assumptions. Identify multi-objective synergies where a probe serves its primary purpose while strengthening reusable infrastructure.
5. **Information-value ranking** -- Evaluate candidate next steps by potential information gain ($\text{downstream leverage} \times \text{assumption criticality}$) rather than conventional urgency.
6. **Bounded proposal** -- Formulate at most one or two concrete next steps with explicit falsification criteria and concise rationales.

## Output contract

A structured planning document containing:

- Inventory of current means, capacities, and fixed constraints.
- Matrix of load-bearing assumptions, associated epistemic dependencies, and proposed cheap probes.
- One or two recommended next steps with information-value justification and stopping conditions.
- Explicit handoff points for decomposition or intake, with no in-session execution.

## When to include

Any initiative facing genuine uncertainty, unclear requirements, high ambiguity, or strategic divergence where executing without validating core assumptions risks expensive wasted work.
