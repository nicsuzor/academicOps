# /plan -- Effectual Planning

Facilitate strategic exploration and structure plans under genuine uncertainty. Plans are hypotheses; execution is downstream of understanding. Use the analytical lenses in `strategize` (altitude, reversibility, effectuation) to ground the inquiry without restating them.

## 1. Non-Execution Boundary

Treat planning as thinking time, not doing time.

- Facilitate thinking rather than implementing solutions mid-stream.
- Do not execute recommended tasks, modify project files, or run operational commands.
- Silently persist surfaced decisions, constraints, and insights using `remember`.
- Stop after presenting guidance; leave execution timing to the caller.

## 2. Thinking-Time Exploration

When exploring direction, structure the conversation around six thinking-time dimensions rather than jumping to solutions:

- **Vision** -- What change do you want to create? What does success look like?
- **Constraints** -- What are the real limitations? Which are fixed versus flexible?
- **Momentum** -- What is already working? Where is the current traction?
- **Fears** -- What failure modes or risks cause concern?
- **Energy** -- Where is engagement and intrinsic motivation highest?
- **Alignment** -- How does this connect to broader objectives and existing commitments?

Receive inputs as fragments rather than polished specifications. Work with scraps gracefully by placing them, linking them, and noting ambiguity as valuable signal.

## 3. Epistemic Mapping & Hypothesis Structuring

Every plan rests on assumptions. Transform static roadmaps into empirical investigations:

- **Surface load-bearing unknowns**: Identify untested assumptions that would invalidate the direction if false.
- **Epistemic dependencies**: Map what must be known before determining what must be done.
- **Design cheap probes**: Prefer small, affordable probes that test critical assumptions before committing resources to heavy implementation.
- **Detect synergies**: Identify actions that simultaneously advance an objective, validate infrastructure, and produce reusable artifacts.
- **Treat surprises as data**: When results diverge from expectation, adapt goals to newly revealed affordances.

## 4. Prioritization by Information Value

When determining next steps, rank by learning potential rather than urgency or perceived importance:

$$\text{information\_value} \approx \text{downstream\_weight} \times \text{assumption\_criticality}$$

Ask: *What action would most improve understanding of what is possible and what matters?* Give priority to nodes that unblock downstream work while resolving high-risk uncertainties.

## 5. Bounded Proposals & Handoff

Bias toward proposing concrete shapes rather than asking open-ended questions:

- Offer at most one or two high-value options with concise rationales instead of presenting an unbounded menu.
- When thinking converges, hand off to downstream workflows:
  - Offer to capture structured tasks via graph intake.
  - Offer to break validated initiatives down via `decompose`.
