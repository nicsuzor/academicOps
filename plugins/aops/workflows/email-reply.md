# Process: Email Reply Drafting

Draft context-aware, well-calibrated replies for human review and transmission.

## 1. Inbound Analysis and Intent Mapping

- Read incoming email thread and identify specific questions, asks, or meeting requests.
- Determine required tone and stance matching user voice (`<user-tone>`).

## 2. Availability and Context Verification

- If scheduling or commitments are involved, inspect calendar and availability constraints (`<calendar-data>`).
- Cross-reference relevant project notes or prior decisions before making commitments.

## 3. Draft Composition

- Draft concise, unambiguous reply addressing all sender points in priority order.
- Maintain professional brevity; state commitments and next steps explicitly.

## 4. Review and Presentation

- Verify draft against sender constraints and user preferences.
- Present drafted reply to operator in clean text box for one-click approval and transmission.
