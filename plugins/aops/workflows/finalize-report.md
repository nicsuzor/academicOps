# Process: Finalize Report

Systematic revision and polish workflow to incorporate feedback into finalized reports.

## 1. Feedback Ingestion and Audit

- Extract all stakeholder and reviewer feedback items into a structured checklist (`<feedback-items>`).
- Prioritize items into critical corrections, substantive revisions, and editorial refinements.

## 2. Report Revision

- Apply required modifications to report text, tables, figures, or data summaries.
- Maintain document consistency across cross-references, executive summary, and appendices.

## 3. Verification of Addressed Items

- Verify each feedback item in the checklist with pinpoint line or figure citations.
- Confirm no regressions or contradictions were introduced by revisions.

## 4. Final Formatting and Sign-off

- Render final output document (e.g. PDF or Markdown) ensuring typography and layout standards.
- Present summary digest of changes to principal for release approval (`wf-signoff-brief`).
