# Process: Reference Letter

Authoring and verification workflow for academic and professional recommendation letters.

## 1. Candidate Intake and Context Assembly

- Assemble candidate CV, transcripts, research statements, and target opportunity details (`<candidate-dossier>`).
- Record recommender capacity, relationship duration, and direct observations of candidate work.

## 2. Key Qualities and Evidence Selection

- Select 2-3 standout qualities (e.g. intellectual independence, analytical depth, collaborative leadership).
- Map each quality to specific, concrete anecdotes or accomplishments from candidate dossier.

## 3. Letter Drafting

- Author recommendation letter following institutional standards and target venue expectations.
- Ensure balanced, highly specific praise with concrete comparative rankings where appropriate.

## 4. Verification and Delivery Preparation

- Verify date, recipient address, candidate name spelling, and formatting requirements.
- Present completed draft to recommender for final review and signature (`wf-signoff-loop`).
