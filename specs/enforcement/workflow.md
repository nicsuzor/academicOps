## Layer 3 — Workflow

A set of PKB tasks/subtasks that make up a more **user-reviewable /
user-directed** unit of work.

- **First layer where we genuinely care about _how_ things are done** — not just
  the outcome. (Contrast Layer 0, where the posture is trust-the-method.)
- The "how" **may change according to the needs of the task at hand** — or it may
  turn out to be a fairly generic loop. _[OPEN: task-specific composition vs a
  generic workflow loop — not yet decided.]_
