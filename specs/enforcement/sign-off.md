## Layer 4 — Sign-off

The final review / approval over the **workflow as a whole unit**.

- **Surface-agnostic.** PR-merge is _one_ instantiation, not the definition. Not
  all academic tasks are done on GitHub — a peer review, for example, is sent out
  manually by email or web form. Sign-off binds to whatever the delivery surface
  is.
- **Independent of in-workflow review.** A project may have review _inside_ the
  workflow and still want a final review over the whole unit — e.g. TJA / Tox
  have some in-workflow review but still want a final review process over the
  work as a whole.
